## Top 5 "Why a website if I have Facebook/Google?" answers
1. **"People Google you before they call — even referrals."** A site confirms you're legit and puts services, hours, and your number one tap away.
2. **"You don't own Facebook."** Reach and rules change overnight, and plenty of customers aren't on it. Your site is the one place you control.
3. **"Your Google profile gets stronger with a site."** A linked website gives Google more to show and gives searchers a reason to pick you over the next listing.
4. **"Google, Yelp and Facebook show your competitors right next to you."** On your site, it's only you.
5. **"It does the job you'd do on the phone."** Booking (salons/gyms), quote requests + gallery (trades), click-to-call + service area (towing/auto) — it works while you're on the job.

Goal of every call: **book the owner call.** Don't sell the website — sell the conversation.
