# Vanguard — Setter Pay Plan

**Structure:** commission only. No pay per booked call, no pay per show.
**Rate:** 8–10% of the website project price, paid when the client pays in full up front.

---

## 1. How setters get paid

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Commission rate** | **8%** of the project price on every deal that closes from the setter's appointment |
| **Rate bump** | **10%** on *all* of that month's deals once the setter reaches **8 closed deals** in a calendar month |
| **What it's paid on** | The website project fee only (Foundation $1,800–$2,200 / Full Presence $2,400–$2,800) |
| **Care Plan / retention** | No commission on the monthly Care Plan (see §6 to change this) |
| **When it's paid** | Next payout after the client's full payment clears |
| **Payout schedule** | Every two weeks, for payments that cleared in that period |

**Per deal:**

| Project price | 8% | 10% |
|---|---|---|
| $1,800 | $144 | $180 |
| $2,300 (average) | $184 | $230 |
| $2,800 | $224 | $280 |

---

## 2. What a setter can earn

Planning numbers from PIPELINE.md: 3 booked calls/day, 70% show, 30% close, $2,300 average project.

```
Booked/week     = 3 × 5          = 15
Shows/week      = 15 × 0.70      = 10.5
Closes/week     = 10.5 × 0.30    = 3.15
Closes/month    = 3.15 × 4.33    ≈ 13.6   → over 8, so the 10% rate applies
Monthly earnings = 13.6 × $2,300 × 10% ≈ $3,130
```

| Month | Closed deals | Rate | Setter earns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ramp-up | 3 | 8% | ~$550 |
| Below bump | 7 | 8% | ~$1,290 |
| At target | 13–14 | 10% | ~$3,130 |

Because clients pay 100% up front, the setter is paid in full on the first payout after the close. There is no waiting for a launch payment.

---

## 3. What it costs the agency

- **8–10% of each project** ($184–$230 on a $2,300 deal). Nothing on appointments that don't close.
- At target (~13.6 deals/month): ~$31K project revenue, ~$3.1K setter commission.
- **Capacity check:** one setter at target sends you 13–14 builds a month. Confirm you can deliver that many inside the 21-Day Launch Guarantee before hiring more setters.

---

## 4. When a deal counts for commission

All must be true. The owner confirms in GHL.

1. The setter booked the appointment and is set as **Setter Assigned** on the opportunity.
2. The lead was DNC-scrubbed before it was dialled, and no texts were sent without consent.
3. The demo existed before the first dial.
4. The client paid the full project price, and the payment cleared.
5. The deal closes within **120 days** of the setter's booked call.

**Clawback:** if the client is refunded within **60 days** of paying, the commission is deducted from the setter's next payout.

**Compliance rule:** any deal where the setter broke the calling or texting rules (no DNC scrub, texts without consent, calling someone on the internal do-not-call list) pays **no commission**. The plan never rewards cutting corners.

## 5. Tracking in GHL

| Field | Set by |
|---|---|
| Setter Assigned (User) | Setter, when booking |
| Payment Collected ($ / date) | Owner or GHL invoice status |
| Refunded ($ / date) | Owner |
| Commission Rate (8% / 10%) | Owner, at month end |

At each payout: filter opportunities with **Payment Collected** dated in the period, no refund, grouped by Setter Assigned. Count each setter's closed deals for the month to set 8% or 10%.

---

## 6. Optional: commission on retention

Not included by default. If you want setters to care about clients staying on the Care Plan, one simple option is **10% of the first 3 months of Care Plan fees** ($45–$75 per client). Decide before hiring, and put it in writing.

---

## 7. Legal / admin to confirm with a lawyer or accountant

- **Contractor vs. employee.** Commission-only usually means 1099 contractors. If a setter is legally an employee (you set their hours, script, tools and supervise closely), minimum-wage rules apply and commission-only may not be allowed.
- **Written plan.** Some states require commission plans in writing, signed by the setter. Use this document as the base.
- **Clawbacks and final pay.** Some states restrict clawbacks, and set rules for paying commission after someone leaves.

---

## 8. One-page summary for new setters

- You earn **8% of every website project** that closes from a call you booked.
- Close **8 or more** in a month and **all** that month's deals pay **10%**.
- Clients pay in full up front, so you're paid in full on the next payout.
- A typical $2,300 project pays you **$184**, or **$230** at 10%.
- At target (about 13–14 closed deals a month) that's roughly **$3,100 a month**.
- **You don't get paid** for bookings that don't close, for deals refunded within 60 days, or for any lead where the calling or texting rules were broken.
- Log every booking with yourself as **Setter Assigned** in GHL, or it can't be credited to you.
