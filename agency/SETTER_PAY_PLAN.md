# Vanguard — Setter Pay Plan

**Structure:** commission only. No pay per booked call, no pay per show.
**Rate:** a flat **$100 per closed deal**, rising to **$125 per deal** for the whole month once you close 8 or more. Paid when the client has paid in full up front.

---

## 1. How setters get paid

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Per deal** | **$100** for every deal that closes from the setter's appointment |
| **Rate bump** | **$125** on *all* of that month's deals once the setter reaches **8 closed deals** in a calendar month |
| **What it's paid on** | Any website project (Foundation $997 or Full Presence $1,497). Same amount for both, so setters never push the bigger package |
| **Care Plan / retention** | No commission on the monthly Care Plan (see §6 to change this) |
| **When it's paid** | Next payout after the client's full payment clears |
| **Payout schedule** | Every two weeks, for payments that cleared in that period |

**Why flat, not a percentage:** at $997 a project, 8–10% would be $80–$100 a deal, which isn't enough to keep good setters. A flat $100–$125 is about 10–12.5% of a Foundation build and keeps the maths simple.

---

## 2. What a setter can earn

Planning numbers from PIPELINE.md: 3 booked calls/day, 70% show, 30% close. These are planning assumptions until we have real data.

```
Booked/week     = 3 × 5          = 15
Shows/week      = 15 × 0.70      = 10.5
Closes/week     = 10.5 × 0.30    = 3.15
Closes/month    = 3.15 × 4.33    ≈ 13.6   → over 8, so $125 per deal applies
Monthly earnings = 13.6 × $125   ≈ $1,700
```

| Month | Closed deals | Per deal | Setter earns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ramp-up | 3 | $100 | ~$300 |
| Below bump | 7 | $100 | ~$700 |
| At target | 13–14 | $125 | ~$1,700 |

Because clients pay 100% up front, the setter is paid in full on the first payout after the close. There is no waiting for a launch payment.

---

## 3. What it costs the agency

- **$100–$125 per closed deal** (about 10–12.5% of a $997 build, 7–8% of a $1,497 build). Nothing on appointments that don't close.
- At target (~13.6 deals/month): ~$13.5K–$20K project revenue, ~$1.7K setter commission.
- **Capacity check:** one setter at target sends you 13–14 builds a month. Confirm you can deliver that many inside the 14-Day Launch Guarantee before hiring more setters.

---

## 4. When a deal counts for commission

All must be true. The owner confirms in GHL.

1. The setter booked the appointment and is set as **Setter Assigned** on the opportunity.
2. The lead was DNC-scrubbed before it was dialled, and no texts were sent without consent.
3. The demo existed before the first dial.
4. The client paid the full project price, and the payment cleared.
5. The deal closes within **120 days** of the setter's booked call.

**Clawback:** if the client is refunded within **60 days** of paying, the commission is deducted from the setter's next payout.

**Compliance rule:** any deal where the setter broke the calling or texting rules (no DNC scrub, texts without consent, calling someone on the internal do-not-call list) pays **no commission**. The plan never rewards cutting corners.

---

## 5. Tracking in GHL

| Field | Set by |
|---|---|
| Setter Assigned | Setter, when booking |
| Payment Collected Amount / Date (opportunity) | Owner or GHL invoice status |
| Refunded Amount / Date (opportunity) | Owner |
| Commission Rate (opportunity: $100 / $125) | Owner, at month end |

At each payout: filter opportunities with **Payment Collected Date** in the period, no refund, grouped by Setter Assigned. Count each setter's closed deals for the month to set $100 or $125.

---

## 6. Optional: commission on retention

Not included by default. If you want setters to care about clients staying on the Care Plan, one simple option is **$25 when a client they booked stays on the Care Plan for 3 months**. Decide before hiring, and put it in writing.

---

## 7. Legal / admin to confirm with a lawyer or accountant

- **Contractor vs. employee.** Commission-only usually means 1099 contractors (or overseas contractors with a W-8BEN). If a setter is legally an employee (you set their hours, script, tools and supervise closely), minimum-wage rules apply and commission-only may not be allowed.
- **Written plan.** Some states require commission plans in writing, signed by the setter. Use this document as the base.
- **Clawbacks and final pay.** Some states restrict clawbacks, and set rules for paying commission after someone leaves.

---

## 8. One-page summary for new setters

- You earn **$100 for every website project** that closes from a call you booked.
- Close **8 or more** in a month and **all** that month's deals pay **$125**.
- Clients pay in full up front, so you're paid in full on the next payout.
- At target (about 13–14 closed deals a month) that's roughly **$1,700 a month**.
- **You don't get paid** for bookings that don't close, for deals refunded within 60 days, or for any lead where the calling or texting rules were broken.
- Log every booking with yourself as **Setter Assigned** in GHL, or it can't be credited to you.
