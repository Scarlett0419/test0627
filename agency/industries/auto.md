# Industry Playbook — Auto
**Covers:** auto repair, body shops/collision, detailing (shop and mobile), towing
**Use with:** SETTER_SCRIPT.md, CLOSER_SCRIPT.md, DEMO_PLAYBOOK.md, OFFER.md
> No market statistics here on purpose. Anything numeric you say must come from the prospect or a source you checked.

---

## 1. How They Get Customers Today
- **Repeat customers and referrals** — many shops have served the same families for years.
- **Google Maps / GBP** — "mechanic near me," "tow truck near me" calls straight off the listing.
- **Yelp, directories (YP, BBB), Facebook.**
- **Insurance and dealer relationships** (body shops), **motor-club / police rotation lists** (towing — varies by area, ask them).
- **Drive-by / signage** — shop location on a busy road.
- Detailers: Instagram/Facebook before-and-after posts, word of mouth.

**Why no website:** long-established shops who "never needed one," owner is a technician not a marketer, relies on location and loyal regulars, Facebook feels like enough.

**Decision-maker:** owner, often working the bays or behind the counter. Front desk/service writer answers — be polite, ask for the owner by name, ask when he's off the lift.
**When reachable:** right at opening (before bays fill), mid-afternoon lull, near closing. Avoid morning drop-off rush and lunch pickups. Towing: owner/dispatcher is reachable any time but may cut you off for a call — offer a callback.

## 2. What the Website Must Do
| Must-have | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Click-to-call** top of mobile | Towing: "Call Now — 24/7 Towing" only if they truly run 24/7 |
| **Services list** (brakes, A/C, diagnostics, etc.) | Their words from GBP/YP |
| **Hours + map + address** | Most visitors want exactly this |
| **Request appointment / estimate form** | Body shops: photo upload of damage |
| **Before/after gallery** | Essential for body shops and detailers |
| **Reviews** | Quote Google reviews with permission |
| **Trust badges** (certifications, BBB, warranty) | Only what they hold and you verify — don't claim ASE/I-CAR/OEM certification unless confirmed |
| **Service area** | Towing and mobile detailing |
| **Insurance / financing / fleet** notes | Only if offered |

**Tier fit:** Foundation — mobile detailers, single-truck towing, small repair shops. Full Presence — multi-bay repair, collision centers (gallery + estimate upload), detailing with packages, towing with multiple services (roadside, lockout, flatbed).

## 3. Owner Language & Pains
Say: "cars in the bay," "comebacks," "tickets," "shop rate," "customers price-shopping on the phone," "insurance jobs," "calls at 2am."
Pains: phone-tag answering price questions; looking old next to dealer/chain sites; losing younger customers who search online; tow calls going to whoever shows up first on Maps; detailers trapped in DMs.

### SPIN Questions
1. **S:** "How do new customers find you — regulars, Google, Yelp, insurance referrals?"
2. **P:** "How much time does the front desk spend answering 'are you open / do you do X / how much' calls?"
3. **P (towing):** "When a call comes in at night and you don't pick up, where does that customer go?"
4. **I:** "Someone new moves to town and searches for a shop — they see a chain with a full website and you with a map pin. What happens?"
5. **I (body):** "How many estimates do you drive or wait for that could have been screened with photos first?"
6. **I:** "When your longtime regulars retire or move, who replaces them?"
7. **N:** "If people could see your services, hours and reviews and request an appointment without calling, what would that free up?"
8. **N (detailing):** "If your packages and before/afters were on one link you could send instead of answering DMs, how many more bookings could you handle?"

## 4. Objections
**"I've been here 30 years, everybody knows me."**
> "That's the story we'd put front and center. The people who already know you don't need a site — the new ones in town do, and they look online first."

**"I'm too busy / bays are full."**
> "Great problem. A site can show hours and services so the phone stops with the same questions, and it gives you fill-in work when it slows down."

**"Word of mouth is enough."**
> "When someone gets your name from a friend, they search it. Right now they get a map pin. The site closes the referral."

**"I'm not a computer guy."**
> "You don't touch it. We build, launch, and maintain it. You send us a few shop photos."

**"I get my calls from the motor club / insurance."** (towing/body)
> "Keep those. This is the business that's only yours — people who choose you directly."

**"Too expensive."**
> "What's one average repair ticket or tow worth? One or two new regulars covers it."

## 5. Setter Openers
**A — heritage:**
> "Hey [Name], [Setter] with Vanguard. I came across [Shop] — looks like you've been in [city] a long time [only if verified] — and noticed there's no website, just the listings. We built you a demo already. Can the owner spare 15 minutes this week to see it?"

**B — towing:**
> "I'll be quick in case a call comes in. People searching for a tow at night find whoever has a site with a big call button. We built one for [Business] — want me to send it and grab 15 minutes later?"

**C — detailing:**
> "Your before-and-afters on Facebook are great. We turned them into a demo site with your packages so people can book without DMing. Got 15 minutes?"

Talking points: less phone-tag, look as established as you are, click-to-call. Don't: claim certifications, promise to beat the dealer on Google, or criticise their shop.

## 6. Demo Guidance
- **Preset:** `other` (default CTA "Get in Touch"). There's no auto preset. Use `construction` only for body shops if you want "Get a Free Quote."
- **CTA wording ideas (for the call or a future preset):** "Schedule Service," "Get a Free Estimate," "Call Now — 24/7 Towing" (only if true), "Book a Detail."
- **Tagline:** from GBP/Facebook; else one plain line e.g. "Honest auto repair in [City]."
- **Services:** 4–8 from their YP/GBP list.
- **--highlights (verified only):** year established (sources often conflict for old shops — if they do, omit), Google rating + count as shown, BBB accreditation only if seen on bbb.org, certifications only if verified.

## 7. Seasonality, Payment, Red Flags
- **Repair:** A/C in heat, batteries/heating/brakes in cold — broadly year-round.
- **Towing:** busier in winter weather and storms (regional); sell in calmer months or during a daytime lull.
- **Detailing:** busier in spring/summer and holidays; sell in winter.
- **Body shops:** tied to weather/accidents; ask when they're slow.

**Paying upfront:** established shops usually can; one-truck towers and new mobile detailers may be tight — Foundation tier.
**Red flags:** want fake reviews or "certified" claims they can't back; operating unclear (no address, no hours); wants us to list prices they won't honour; will not provide any photos.

---

## 8. Upsell / Care Plan
- Care Plan: hours/holiday updates, seasonal specials (A/C check, winter prep), new gallery photos.
- Online appointment request integration; estimate photo upload (body shops).
- Detailing: packages page + booking tool.
- Fleet/commercial accounts page.
- Review-request link, GBP upkeep.
