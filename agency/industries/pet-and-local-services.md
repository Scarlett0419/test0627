# Industry Playbook — Pet & Local Services
**Covers:** pet grooming/boarding, cleaning/janitorial, daycare/childcare, tailors/alterations, photographers, small restaurants/caterers/food trucks
**Use with:** SETTER_SCRIPT.md, CLOSER_SCRIPT.md, DEMO_PLAYBOOK.md, OFFER.md
> No market statistics here on purpose. Anything numeric you say must come from the prospect or a source you checked.

---

## 1. How They Get Customers Today
| Niche | Main channels |
|---|---|
| Pet grooming/boarding | Regulars, referrals, Facebook/Instagram pet photos, Google Maps, vet referrals |
| Cleaning/janitorial | Referrals, Thumbtack/Angi/Nextdoor, Facebook groups, commercial contracts via networking |
| Daycare | Word of mouth among parents, Facebook, local listings/state directories, churches |
| Tailors | Walk-ins, dry cleaners, bridal shops, Google Maps |
| Photographers | Instagram, referrals, wedding/event platforms |
| Restaurants/caterers/food trucks | Walk-ins, Instagram/Facebook, delivery apps, Google Maps; food trucks post location daily on social |

**Why no website:** social feels like enough; owner does everything; booking is by phone/DM; small or new; English may be a second language for some (tailors, food) — be patient and clear.

**Decision-maker & timing:**
- Groomers: owner is often grooming — call at opening or late afternoon.
- Cleaners: owner may be on jobs; early morning or evening.
- Daycare: director/owner; avoid drop-off and pickup; nap time (early afternoon) is best.
- Tailors: usually in the shop; mid-morning weekdays.
- Photographers: flexible; avoid weekends (shoots).
- Restaurants: between lunch and dinner (roughly 2–4pm), never during service. Food trucks: before they open.

## 2. What the Website Must Do
| Niche | Must-haves |
|---|---|
| Pet | Service menu (bath, full groom, nails, boarding), online booking/request, gallery of pets, vaccination requirements *as the owner states them*, reviews |
| Cleaning | Free quote form (home size / commercial), service area, residential vs commercial, "insured/bonded" only if confirmed, reviews |
| Daycare | Programs by age, schedule a tour form, photos of space, daily routine, licensing info only if verified on the state's own lookup |
| Tailor | Services + turnaround, hours/map, before/after (hems, suits, bridal), call button |
| Photographer | Portfolio first, packages/"starting at" (their numbers), inquiry form with date |
| Food | Menu (text, not PDF), hours, location/map, order/catering inquiry, food truck schedule/where-we-are, photos |

All need click-to-call on mobile, reviews, and Google-friendly hours/address.

**Tier fit:** Foundation — solo groomers, tailors, residential cleaners, food trucks. Full Presence — boarding facilities, daycares, commercial janitorial, photographers (portfolio-heavy), restaurants with catering.

## 3. Owner Language & Pains
Pets: "furbabies," "matted," "no-shows," "booked out weeks." Cleaning: "walkthrough," "recurring clients," "Thumbtack fees." Daycare: "openings," "tours," "parents." Photographers: "sessions," "inquiries," "ghosted." Food: "covers," "the rush," "catering orders," "app fees."
Common pains: DMs/phone all day; no-shows; delivery/lead platform fees; looking amateur to higher-paying clients; nowhere to send people.

### SPIN Questions
1. **S:** "How do most new clients reach you — phone, DMs, an app, walk-in?"
2. **P:** "How much of your day goes to answering the same questions — prices, hours, availability?"
3. **P (cleaning/photo):** "What do Thumbtack or other platforms charge you, and how many of those leads actually book?"
4. **P (food):** "How do catering inquiries reach you now — and do any slip through?"
5. **I:** "When a parent/pet owner compares you to a place with a full website and tour/booking form, how do you think you look?"
6. **I:** "If you miss a call while you're mid-groom or mid-shift, does that person call back or move on?"
7. **N:** "If your menu, prices and booking lived on one link you could put in your Instagram bio, what would that change?"
8. **N (janitorial):** "If an office manager could see you're established and request a walkthrough online, would that help land bigger contracts?"

## 4. Objections
**"I'm booked solid."** > "Then the site's job is fewer questions and better clients — and it lets you raise prices or fill cancellations."
**"Instagram/Facebook is enough."** > "Keep it — the site is the link in your bio that actually books. Not everyone uses Instagram, and Google shows websites."
**"Word of mouth is enough."** > "Referrals look you up first. Give them somewhere to land."
**"I use Thumbtack/delivery apps."** > "Keep them. This is the channel with no per-lead or per-order fee."
**"Not a computer person."** > "We do all of it. Send photos from your phone; we handle the rest and updates."
**"Too expensive / I'm small."** > "What's one recurring client or catering order worth over a year? Foundation tier is built for businesses your size."

## 5. Setter Openers
**Pet:** "Hey [Name], I found [Business] on Google — the pet photos are adorable — but no website. We built a demo with your grooming menu and a booking button. 15 minutes this week?"
**Cleaning:** "We built [Business] a demo site with a free-quote form so clients can request a clean without calling while you're on a job. Can I show you in 15?"
**Daycare:** "Parents usually look online before touring. We built [Business] a demo with a 'Schedule a Tour' button. Could the director spare 15 minutes?"
**Tailor:** "People searching 'alterations near me' find the shops with websites. We built [Business] one — can I show you?"
**Photographer:** "Your Instagram work is great; we built it into a portfolio site with an inquiry form. 15 minutes?"
**Food:** (call 2–4pm) "We built [Business] a demo with your menu, hours and a catering request form. When's a quiet 15 minutes — before service?"

Don't: claim a daycare is licensed, promise delivery-app savings figures, or call during meal rushes/drop-off.

## 6. Demo Guidance
- **Preset:** `other` (CTA "Get in Touch") for all. Use `construction` only for cleaning/janitorial if you want "Get a Free Quote." Pet groomers can use `beauty` if the tone fits (CTA "Book Your Session").
- **CTA wording ideas:** "Book a Groom," "Get a Free Cleaning Quote," "Schedule a Tour," "Check Availability," "Order Catering," "Find the Truck."
- **Services:** 4–8 from GBP/Facebook/menu.
- **--highlights (verified only):** Google rating + count as shown, years open if confirmed, "Family-owned" only if stated. Never licensing or health-inspection claims without a verified official source.

## 7. Seasonality, Payment, Red Flags
- Pet boarding: peaks around holidays and summer travel — sell off-peak. Grooming: pre-holiday rush.
- Cleaning: spring cleaning and move-out periods; commercial is steadier.
- Daycare: enrollment pushes before school year — sell in spring/early summer.
- Tailors: wedding/prom and holiday seasons.
- Photographers: wedding season and fall family sessions — sell in winter.
- Food trucks: slow in cold months in many regions; restaurants vary.

**Paying upfront:** mixed. Food trucks, new cleaners and solo groomers can be cash-tight — lead with Foundation, don't discount. Established daycares, boarding and janitorial usually fine.
**Red flags:** want false licensing/insurance claims; no consistent hours or location; "pay you when it works"; wants us to handle health/licensing compliance content we can't verify; owner can't be reached for decisions.

## 8. Upsell / Care Plan
- Care Plan: menu/price changes, seasonal specials, holiday hours, food truck weekly schedule, new portfolio/pet photos.
- Booking integration (grooming, photography, cleaning quotes).
- Catering page with inquiry form; daycare enrollment/waitlist form.
- Gift cards / packages page; review-request link; GBP upkeep.
