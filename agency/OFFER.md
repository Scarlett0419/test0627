## 1. Value Equation Pass

> `Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)`

### Dream Outcome
**"A website that makes your business look as good as it actually is — live in 21 days, attracting the right clients on autopilot."**

**Who it's for:** US local service businesses with **no website or a weak one** — barbershops/salons/nail/spa, gyms/CrossFit/martial arts/yoga, trades (roofing, fencing, painting, plumbing, HVAC, concrete, landscaping), auto repair/towing/detailing, pet grooming, cleaning, daycare, tailors, photographers, small restaurants. Many have *no site at all* — only a Google Business Profile, Yelp, or Facebook page.

| Dimension | Current State (Pain) | Dream State |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------|
| **Existence** | No website at all — only Google/Yelp/Facebook; customers who search can't find services, prices, hours, or a way to book | A real home base they own, that shows up in search and turns lookers into calls/bookings |
| **Appearance** | Outdated, generic, Wix-tier site that embarrasses them when they send the link | A polished, custom site they're proud to share — one that reflects the real quality of their work |
| **Trust / Credibility** | Prospects can't tell if they're legit | Visitors immediately trust them and inquire |
| **Lead Quality** | Tire-kickers, price shoppers, wrong-fit clients | Pre-qualified, ready-to-buy leads who already see the value |
| **Time** | Months of back-and-forth with cheap freelancers or doing it themselves | Done-for-you in 21 days, no chasing |
| **Effort** | Owner has to manage designers, write copy, source photos | Owner hands off and focuses on their work |

| Lever | How Vanguard Moves It |

## 4. Pricing Structure Recommendation

### Primary: Project-Based (One-Time Fee)

**Base Package — "The Foundation":** $1,800–$2,200
- 5-page custom site
- All stack components above
- Best for: solo providers and no-website businesses — barbers, nail techs, groomers, cleaners, tailors, small trades crews

**Growth Package — "The Full Presence":** $2,400–$2,800
- 7–8 pages + booking integrations, gallery, testimonials section
- Best for: gyms/studios, salons/spas, larger trades and auto shops, restaurants, multi-service businesses

**Payment Terms:**
- 100% up front, paid when the contract is signed, before work begins
- Care Plan billed monthly from launch (optional retention)

> *Rationale: Project-based pricing is clean, easy to close on a call, and aligns with how local/service businesses think. Avoid hourly — it signals commodity and invites nickel-and-diming.*

---

### Optional Add-On: Monthly Care Plan (Retainer)

**Care Plan — $150–$250/month** (month-to-month, cancel anytime)

Includes:
- Hosting management
- Security updates & backups
- Up to 1 hour of content updates/month (swap photos, update hours, add a service)
- Priority response (48-hour turnaround)

> *Rationale: Care Plan converts a one-time project into recurring revenue without requiring the owner to hire a team. Even 5 care plan clients = $750–$1,250/month baseline. Position it as "so you never have to think about it again."*

## 5. Guarantee Structure

### Primary Guarantee: **The 21-Day Launch Guarantee**

> *"If your site isn't live within 21 days of your kick-off call — and you've provided your feedback on schedule — your first month of the Care Plan is on us. No asterisks, no arguments."*

**Why this works:**
- Effort-based (tied to a process Vanguard controls)
- 21 days is genuinely achievable for a one-person shop — it's not a lie
- The penalty (one free Care Plan month, ~$150–$250) is low cost to deliver, high confidence signal to the buyer
- It reframes the risk: the prospect worries the project will drag on forever. This guarantee kills that objection.

### Secondary Guarantee: **"Love It or We Fix It" Design Policy**

> *"If after the first design preview you feel we've missed the mark entirely on the look and feel, say so. We'll reset the visual direction and rebuild it — not just tweak colors. Your investment doesn't move."*

**Why this works:**
- Removes the #1 creative fear: "What if I hate it?"
- This is an effort-based guarantee — Vanguard controls the effort, not the outcome
- In practice, a structured kick-off call makes this almost never trigger — but it eliminates hesitation at the point of close

> **Avoid outcome-based guarantees** ("We guarantee you'll get X new clients from the site") — these are outside Vanguard's control and create liability. Stick to effort and process guarantees.
